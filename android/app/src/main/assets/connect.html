Connect to your PC

This panel is driven by the bridge program running on your gaming PC. Tablet and PC must be on the same Wi-Fi.

Where do I get the address and PIN?
  1. On the PC, start the Truck Deck bridge. Its window shows PC address and PIN.
  2. Type them above, or tap Find my PC and enter only the PIN.
  3. No address in the window? On the PC press Windows + R, type cmd, Enter, then type ipconfig. Use the number beside IPv4 Address.
